Yogurt math

Every recipe says "a spoonful of starter, incubate overnight" - and your liter of milk is not their liter, your spoonful is not their percentage, and overnight is not a number.

Run the batch before the heat

The problem it solves

Starter is a percentage, not a spoonful: about 2% of your milk by volume is the labeled norm, and guessing light stalls the set while guessing heavy wastes culture. The window is a range, not "overnight": 6 to 14 hours depending on how mild or tangy you want it, and the clock starts when it reaches temperature, not when you remember it.

Then the straining question: how much greek do you actually get from a kilo of plain yogurt? About 55% - and labneh closer to 35%, with the rest leaving as whey you can bake with. Yogurt math runs all of it: starter grams and yield for your batch, kept yogurt and whey for your straining, and a window check for the jar still in the incubator.
