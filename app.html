Recipes say "a spoonful of starter, incubate overnight" and stop there. Starter percentage, incubation window and straining yield are multiplication. Run yours before the milk comes off the heat.

Dairy basics still apply. Heat the milk first, cool it before adding starter (hot milk kills the culture - below about 46C / 115F, labeled norm), use clean jars, and chill the finished yogurt promptly. Mold, pink or yeasty smells mean discard the batch. These percentages and windows are labeled published norms, not safety verdicts.
